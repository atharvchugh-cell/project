Where the simulators fit

The stock simulator covers markets, risk and diversification. The real estate game covers illiquid assets, leverage and holding costs. The Sandbox lets a class ask counterfactual questions — what if the 2008 crash had been twice as deep — and watch the timeline change.

All three use virtual money. Nothing here is financial advice; see the methodology page for exactly how the numbers are produced and simplified.
