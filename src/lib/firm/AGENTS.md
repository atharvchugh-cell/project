# Firm career rules

- Firm career balances (firm, founder) derive only from the `src/lib/firm/journal.ts` transaction list; fund fee cash reaches the firm only as the delta of the fund's cumulative fee payments, and `closeQuarter` is idempotent per `closeId`, so no fee, salary or expense is ever posted twice and firm costs never touch fund NAV.
- Firm Phase 2 systems (staff, client satisfaction, idea pipeline, board motions, events) live in `src/lib/firm/phase2.ts`, run inside `closeQuarter` with a career+quarter seeded RNG, and move money only through the journal or existing fund accounting (`subscribe`/`redeemUnits`); open decisions take their stated default at the next close, so outcomes are deterministic and never bypass the ledger.
- Firm-career quarter plans live in the versioned career save (`Career.plan`, cleared only by `closeQuarter` or explicit discard); preview, review and close all use `planDecision` in `src/lib/firm/plan.ts`, and career payment figures come only from `dueBreakdown`/`quarterEstimate`, so what the player reviews is what settles and no screen shows a different amount due.
- New firm careers set `fund.costRule = 'fund_admin'` (administration/custody/audit only, since the firm pays staff and office); classic funds, converted and v1 careers keep the classic fund cost rule, so existing saves keep their contractual behavior.
- Firm cash reports are rebuilt from the journal by period (`cashBridge`), never from snapshot totals or fund-side counters, so opening + inflows − outflows always equals closing.
- Phase 3 (`phase3.ts`): capital calls fund new units only via `subscribe` at NAV inside `closeQuarter` before the quarter trades (late interest raises NAV without units); acquisitions are firm-owned `company` entities whose purchase, sale, revenue and costs post only to the journal, so fund NAV and firm cash stay separate.
- New Client Contracts funds use `marketRule: 'calendar'` (`src/lib/fund/calendar.ts`): one fixed quarterly path from the shared annual data, compounding exactly to each real year; saves without it keep the seeded split.
- Client relations, rivals and staff revenue (`relations.ts`): staff revenue only splits real fee receipts by contribution (never adds money), rival returns are the completed S&P 500 quarter plus fixed fictional style and seeded luck, and new clients join only through `subscribe` at NAV via a `new_client` decision, so outcomes are deterministic and never read future data.
- Founder salary steps live in `Career.salarySchedule` and become the live salary one close ahead (`applySalarySchedule` → `setFounderSalary`); the forecast reads `salaryAt`, so review, settlement and forecast always agree. Staff ability grows only toward `potential` with tenure and morale; pay changes morale, never skill.
- Phase 4 client money (`phase4.ts`): quarterly contributions, call-driven wires, new clients (half wired, half a Phase 3 commitment) and rival wins/losses enter only through `subscribe`/`redeemUnits` and are logged in `Career.flows`; rival assets (`rivalAum`) change only with their returns and logged pitches, so every client dollar is traceable and never counts as return.
- Firm obligations (`payables.ts`): every bill (payroll, severance, office, business costs, events/bonuses) goes through `payOrOwe`; the unaffordable part stays itemized in `Career.payables` (`arrears` is their total) and `settlePayables` pays them first at the next close in a fixed order, so a shortage never forgives or double-charges a bill.
- Rival transfers (`Career.transfers`): pitch money leaves a rival only when a distinct offer is created and is held pending until accepted (wired + committed) or declined (returned); money lost to a rival reaches it only when the fund pays the redemption, after rival returns for that quarter, so client money never vanishes or earns a quarter it was not invested.
- The closing history record (aum/nav) is rewritten after all Phase 2-4 closing flows; late capital-call interest enters `playQuarter` as `income` after the starting NAV, so NAV, compounded returns and charts agree. Owned-business cash for estimates, forecasts and settlement comes only from `businessQuarter` (integration included).
- Phase 5 (`phase5.ts`): activists, crises and the newspaper run last in `closeQuarter` with a career+quarter seed; they open decisions only while the fund runs and move money only via `redeemUnits` or `payOrOwe`, and newspaper figures are copied from the closing history and `standings`, so headlines, rankings and fund returns always agree.
- Wealth phase (`life.ts`): possessions, upkeep, life actions/events, relationships, reputation and the firm office tier live in `Career.life`; purchases and upkeep post only from the `founder` entity (never firm or fund cash), unpaid upkeep stays in `life.owed` and two short closes force a sale; one copy per item and perks take the best owned value, so nothing stacks or flips for profit in the purchase quarter.
- Ownership and endings (`exit.ts`): the cap table in `Career.ownership` sums to 100%; partner buy-ins post `equity_sale` into the firm, distributions split by stake via `postDistribution`, and sales/IPO/retirement pay the equity owner through the journal and set `Career.ending` + `status: 'ended'` once, never touching fund assets. `valuation` is consolidated (firm cash + owned-company cash − bills − business loans + earnings multiple), so internal retention/distribution, loan draws and principal repayments never change equity value.
- Quarter flow: the primary action is one-click "Simulate quarter" (same `closeQuarter`, ref + time guard against double runs) with an inline plan strip and a dismissible result card; "Review plan" stays optional. The forecast view (`FirmForecast.tsx`) uses `cashForecast` what-ifs and separate balance and flow scales.
- Client book (`clients.ts`): every client (and each prospect, keyed `prospect:<decision>`) has a saved profile; every inflow (contribution, call bonus, top-up, meeting offer, new client, referral, rival win) is capped by `roomFor` = capacity − (units at NAV + uncalled commitments + open offers), and offers are sized only by `quote` from completed history, so one allocation is never funded twice and repeated asks cannot print money. Opt-in management contracts settle through fund fee liabilities; lockups, mandates and promises remain negotiable. Reviews redeem only through `redeemUnits` after lockup.
- Businesses (`business.ts`): `businessQuarter` is the single projection for estimates, forecast and close (levers are dated effects, loans amortize straight-line, company cash retained in the journal under the business id pays its loan first); every close saves a statement using the revenue actually posted, and sale/closure/foreclosure follow the waterfall with unpaid amounts staying in `payables`.
- Deals (`deals.ts`): offer → counter/reject/lost → agreed → paid diligence → explicit close; nothing is paid before closing, each deal closes once and expires after two quarters. Business deals are cash-free/debt-free with a saved `Subsidiary.closing` bridge (seller keeps pre-closing cash), and research-shop benefits apply once at closing into `Subsidiary.benefit`. Rival-manager deals pay firm cash for the fee business only; consenting mandates subscribe at NAV, the rival joins `acquiredRivals` and leaves standings, rival returns and pitches.
- Governance (`governance.ts`): constitution rules act through `constitutionOrders` (planDecision), `reserveQuartersOf` (reserveTarget), `payoutCap` (maxDistribution), premium service (close cost and client reviews) and the vote threshold; amendments vote at the next close by cap-table weight, big moves (manager deals, sale of control, IPO filing, succession) need `approval`, and old saves get a no-limit default so their behavior is unchanged.

- Staff attribution (`attribution.ts`): the fund engine records per-order receipts only when `fund.receipts` exists (career saves); every approved idea's fill, lot share, sales and costs come from those receipts, sleeves count live lot value plus pending orders (reviews free nothing; only real sales do) under one 15% firm-wide cap, and the one-year idea review stays a separate hypothetical, so staff P&L always reconciles to the actual fund. Lots carry `basisLeft`/`realized`: each real sale releases basis pro rata and books realized profit at sale time, so later prices only move unrealized.
- Staff research (`analysis.ts`) reads only completed quarters; skill bounds screening breadth, analytical error, certainty and crowding discipline with a fixed random-draw count, so ability changes the process measurably without future data or changed prices.
- Constitution position limits are enforced at execution (`QuarterDecision.maxPosition` in `applyOrders`) over the combined stream (desk, ideas, delegated, deal calls), and preview uses the same stream, so no entry can exceed the policy.
- Every transaction posted during `closeQuarter` (defaults, life events, forced sales) is stamped with that close's id; life code re-reads `n.life` after any call that returns a new career, future time promises live in `life.promises`, and possession `price` is immutable (wear is a separate multiplier).
- Client money offers save exact `wireNow`/`commitLater`/`expiresQ` terms in the decision payload; acceptance settles those terms via `settlementOf` (scaled together only if capacity shrank, and logged), so quote, preview, subscription, commitment and history agree.
- Deal `closeCheck` uses one funds-and-obligations bridge (cash, loan, price, integration, reserve, existing arrears and diligence liabilities) for both UI and `closeDeal`, and may suggest a lender-limited loan that funds the close.
- Legacy attribution lots freeze their estimated basis/realized split from receipt pre-trade value before fraction dilution or sale; prior net proceeds remain in total P&L and the estimated marker persists, so new executions cannot erase historical profit or imply reconstructed sale history.
- Client management fee contracts are opt-in fund fields: all client charges use one pre-fee net-asset mark and whole invested units, replacing shared management accrual; liabilities pay from fund assets and only paid manager-entitled fees reach the firm, while legacy saves retain shared fees until explicit opt-in.
- Manager facilities and business refinancing use the shared `loanSchedule`; refinance journals a new draw, old payoff and costs atomically without forgiving arrears/defaults, while manager covenants use paid mandate-fee earnings and default transfers fee entitlement to the lender, never client units or assets.
- Newspaper coverage saves seeded, bounded, expiring mood modifiers used by contributions and rival pitches; opt-in `newsFromYear` sector sentiment changes only quarterly stock timing with zero annual log shock, never annual endpoints or the benchmark; old saves activate only at a year boundary and confidence keeps its profit/payment rule.
- All-client sessions delegate eligible portfolio reviews to investor relations, pay itemized service cost from firm cash and honor saved review/meeting cooldowns; individual founder meetings retain their time rules, preventing a batch button from creating free money or repeat satisfaction.
- Owned-company charts read journal cash and saved statements; profit deducts accrued interest, never principal, and full cash retention is an operating lever through the existing settlement projection, so company growth never enters fund NAV.
- Career immersion is an optional save flag: outside employee retainers use a seeded ability/morale quote (independent of salary), posted once from external clients as `staff_revenue`, separate from attributed fund fees; forecast, estimates and valuation include it without touching fund assets. Immediate desk trades use `applyOrders` with constitution limits and `applyFundAction` receipts, never advance time.
- Performance-backed fame requires four valid completed trailing quarters with compounded net return above the benchmark and no late payments; it boosts capacity-limited quotes and approach probability, pressures only trailing rivals through expiring coverage, and raises incoming manager staff retention pay used by close checks and payroll. Legacy careers retain neutral modifiers; subscriptions and salary settlement use existing accounting.
- Listed-firm valuation applies bounded completed net-return/benchmark and managed-asset scale modifiers to trailing earnings only; consolidated company cash minus debt stays unscaled, client AUM never becomes owned equity, and unlisted firms retain their prior valuation rule.
- Faces (`faces.ts`) derive stable legacy-compatible traits from identity seeds, with age/mood/role as presentation-only inputs, so a portrait never changes accounting or simulation randomness.
- Brands (`brand.ts`) live in optional versioned `Career.empire`, explicitly upgraded for existing careers; rebrands charge only the owning company account, retain entity IDs and create bounded expiring offer/revenue campaigns, preventing balance migration, repeat charges or permanent promotional growth.
- `Logo.tsx` renders fictional brands from stable entity IDs and semantic industry palettes; `Rebrand.tsx` uses only validated brand actions and owning-account costs, so presentation cannot bypass affordability or alter ownership.
- Identity lookup (`identity.ts`) is presentation-only and resolves stable person/company IDs before organisation fallbacks, including rivals, founder aliases and historical rebrand receipts; `SenderIdentity.tsx` shares that lookup across opening/resume, notices, decisions and newspaper without changing saves or simulation randomness.
- Public management (`publicBook.ts`, `publicFirm.ts`) is optional and legacy listings explicitly activate it: actual share counts reconcile to cap-table percentages; primary proceeds fund the firm, secondary shares use founder cash and purchased-lot basis, dividends/buybacks reduce equity cash, corporate principal stays value-neutral and interest/payables settle via the shared loan schedule. Own-manager fund investment is prohibited to avoid circular NAV; public news uses a separate seeded stream and revalues only the earnings multiple, never historical tickers. Founder control sales release purchased basis pro rata; private partner buyouts are disabled on the public ledger until they maintain share basis.
- Approved ideas are target weights (`ideaOrders` in `plan.ts`): each buys up to `size × AUM` minus the current position, and other holdings are sold evenly when fund cash after desk trades and next quarter's dues falls short, so the chosen weight is reached in both preview and close.
- `buyNow`/`closeAgreedNow` (`deals.ts`) skip negotiation and diligence but still use `closeCheck`/`closeDeal` (auto-sized lender loan), so reserves, votes and journal posting are unchanged. Celebrity investors (`celebrityAtClose`) and auto-accept (`autoAcceptMoney` → `acceptAllMoney`) only create or accept ordinary `new_client`/`top_up` decisions, so every dollar still enters via `subscribe` at NAV.
- Founder share sales may sell founder shares after lockup (bought lots first); stock splits rescale shares, price history and per-share figures without moving cash. Rich-list rows are presentation only.
- Personal ventures (`ventures.ts`): businesses bought with founder cash live in `Career.life.ventures`; purchase, expansion, profit (paid once per close via `venturesAtClose`) and sale post only between founder and external, and their value counts in net worth, so firm cash and fund NAV never move. Firm subsidiary expansion (`expandBusiness`) is saved as dated effects so forecasts and the close agree.
- Founder setup (`founderSetup.ts`) owns optional identity/background state and the dependency-light family-bill projection shared by personal forecasts; old careers opt in without opening cash or loan changes, preventing circular engine initialization and balance migration.
- Founder life (`founder.ts`) uses seeded quarter/person decisions, shared time/cooldowns and founder/spouse journal accounts; adult paths persist as family contacts, and ended careers cannot resolve pending personal decisions, preserving accounting and ending finality.
