# Deferred firm-career expansion

## Scope and safeguards
Complete consequential newspaper coverage, client-specific management fees, financed rival-manager purchases, and business refinancing. Preserve the completed career systems, one-click quarters, free guest play, other modes, existing saves, and separate fund/firm/founder/company accounts. Do not publish, use runtime AI, or change stock return data. Use disposable careers for verification, never the Harbor Hill save.

## 1. Historical prices and consequential news
- Use the existing shared annual stock and index data. Verify every overlapping stock/year across the normal game and fund, and compound the four fund quarters with `(1 + q1)…(1 + q4) - 1`, not by adding percentages.
- Keep the current fixed calendar path and annual endpoints. Historical news sentiment is reflected in that price path and reported as events occur. Fictional coverage changes client mood, reputation, and subsequent rival pitch/retention outcomes, not historical stock returns. Player press choices cannot change historical prices or reveal future results.
- Add saved, bounded, expiring mood/coverage effects and clear stories identifying affected clients and rivals. Keep investor confidence governed by profit and payment rules, separate from temporary client mood.
- Explain Akamai availability using existing listing/IPO anchors rather than inventing or replacing returns. Verify its first tradable annual year explicitly.

## 2. Fund-paid client management fees
- Apply each client's agreed annual management rate quarterly to their whole invested balance, including contributed capital. This replaces that client's share of the shared management fee; it is not an extra contribution charge.
- Default to the existing disclosed 1% annual rate. Add negotiated, bounded rates with explicit acceptance and effective dates. Founder capital retains its existing treatment unless changed through a disclosed contract.
- Deduct fees from fund assets and credit the firm only when actually paid. Scheduled contributions remain capital, subscribe at NAV, respect client capacity, and never count as investment profit.
- Preserve the existing annual 10% performance-fee/high-water-mark contract; this work does not introduce separate negotiated performance fees.
- Keep client charges, aggregate fund liabilities, fee receipts, firm journal, recap, Overview, and runway consistent. Show contracted versus uncertain contributions and fee estimates clearly.
- Existing saves retain shared fees until an explicit fee-contract migration that preserves balances, units and accrued liabilities. New careers enable client terms by default.

## 3. Loans for rival-manager purchases
- Offer purchase-price financing subject to the existing lender price/earnings limits, funding bridge, three-quarter reserve, and governance approval.
- Base lender earnings on the fee business, never client assets. Loan cash and debt belong to the firm; consenting client mandates still enter the fund at NAV and cannot finance the purchase.
- Reuse business-loan interest, amortization, maturity, debt-service covenant, penalty and default rules. Identify the acquired fee business securing the facility and implement an explicit enforceable default consequence without confiscating client assets.
- Show purchase funding, debt service, covenant headroom and liabilities in deal review, Overview, company/manager records, valuation, and runway.

## 4. Business refinancing and operations
- Add lender offers with different rates and terms to existing owned-company loan controls. Preview the new draw, old balance payoff, transaction costs, cash required, remaining debt and reserve before confirmation.
- Settle draw and payoff atomically through the existing journal. Record immutable old/new facility history, prevent duplicate execution, and retain unpaid interest/bills. Refinancing must not erase a default or bypass lender checks.
- Reuse the same interest, amortization, maturity and covenant projection as original loans. Do not create value from principal draws/repayments.
- Preserve current business pricing/capacity/reinvestment/retention controls and their real posted growth/cash statements; update those statements and forecasts for refinanced debt rather than adding an unrelated business phase.
